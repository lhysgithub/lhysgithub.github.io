Research Interests

I am currently an Assistant Researcher and Postdoctoral Fellow (co-advised by Prof. Liehuang Zhu) at the Beijing Institute of Technology. My research primarily focuses on microservices, blockchain systems, anomaly detection, root cause analysis, and adversarial examples.

😃I am always open to collaborations on research topics about AI4RE(Artificial Intelligence for Reliability)!
